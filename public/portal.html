Before you start

Please review the Foodol Restaurant Agreement. The key points:

  • Your data is yours. Your menu and guest list belong to you. Download them anytime.
  • We never sell your data or share your guest list with other restaurants.
  • POS access is limited. If you connect Square or Clover, Foodol only reads your menu and adds Foodol orders. It can’t see your other sales, customers, staff or bank details.
  • Payments go to you through your own Square or Clover account. Foodol takes no commission.
  • Leave anytime. Ask and we delete your data within 7 days and confirm in writing.

Popular dishes

What guests can crave

The choices on Foodol’s “What are you craving?” screen (up to 6, plus “Surprise me”). Tag each dish with these in your Menu tab.

How Foodol picks combos
  1. Each guest gets a main from what they’re craving. Your popular dishes come first, in the order above.
  2. The Crowd Pleaser uses your most popular dishes. The Smart Deal is the cheapest well-liked order that still earns the free item. The Adventure uses your next-best dishes so guests try something different. The Feast is for big appetites, with an extra side, and The Wildcard shows off dishes none of the others used.
  3. Groups get shared sides that go with their food (fries with sandwiches, curry puffs with Thai). Desserts only appear when a guest picks something sweet.
  4. Every combo aims to reach your free-item minimum, so guests get the reward without having to add anything.
