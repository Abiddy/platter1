How it works

Three taps from “what’s good here?” to ordering.

No app to download, no hardware, and nothing for your staff to learn beyond reading a 4-digit code.

1

Scan

A sticker or poster at the table or counter opens Foodol on the guest’s phone.

2

Foodol picks

Guests say how many people and what they’re craving. Foodol builds combos from your real menu and prices.

3

Free item, bigger order

Orders over your minimum unlock a free item you choose. The guest shows a code at the counter and you ring it up.
