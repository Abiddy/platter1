How it works

From “what’s good here?”
to ordering in three taps

Nothing for your staff to learn beyond reading a 4-digit code.

Scan

A sticker or poster at the table or counter opens Foodol right on the guest’s phone.

Step 2

Foodol picks

Guests say how many people and what they’re craving. Foodol builds combos from your real menu and prices.

Step 3

Free item, bigger order

Orders over your minimum unlock a free item you choose. Guests show a code and you ring it up.
